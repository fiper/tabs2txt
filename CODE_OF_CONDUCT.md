# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Discuss code, not the person who wrote it.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith, and give feedback on the change rather than the author.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a feature without that being a judgement of you.
- Keep discussion in issues and pull requests focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks, and sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.

## Scope

This applies to every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately by email to YOUR_EMAIL. Reports are read by the maintainers only, and the reporter's identity is not shared without their consent.

Maintainers will respond with whatever is proportionate: a private warning, removal of a comment, or a block from the repository. Maintainers who do not uphold this document in good faith can expect the same.
